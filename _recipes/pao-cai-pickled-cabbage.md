## Equipment

Mason jar

## Ingredients

- 1 small cabbage or ½ medium cabbage
- 1 carrot
- 2 tsp salt (to draw out moisture)
- Optional: 3 cloves garlic (blanch for 30s first)
- Optional: 1 dried red chili pepper

### Brine
- 1/2 cup sugar
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 cup rice vinegar
- 1/2 cup white vinegar OR 3/4 cup white vinegar
- 2/3 cup water

## Instructions

1. Tear cabbage leaves into 1.5” x 1.5” sections
2. Cut carrot into matchsticks
3. Add salt to cabbage and carrots, mix and allow to draw out water for 1 hour
4. Combine vinegar, water, sugar, salt, and chili peppers in a small saucepan
5. Heat, stirring occasionally until sugar is dissolved
6. Remove pickle mix from heat and let cool
7. Rinse salted cabbage thoroughly with cold water, transfer drained cabbage to sanitized jar
8. Pour cooled pickle mix into jar, ensuring cabbage is submerged. Use glass weight if necessary
9. Refrigerate for 2 days before eating. Keeps up to 5-10 days
