## Equipment

Instant Pot

## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup dried mung beans
- 3 cups water
- rock sugar to taste

## Instructions

1. Rinse dried mung bean, add to pressure cooker with water and 1/2 the sugar
2. Set pressure cooker for 1 minute on high
3. After 1 minute is done wait 10 minutes natural release
4. Release pressure, add remaining sugar to taste
5. Cool in fridge and enjoy cold

## Notes

*Mung beans cook for approximately 20 minutes (10 minutes to pressure, 1 minute on high, 10 minutes before steam release)
