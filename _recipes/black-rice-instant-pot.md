## Equipment

- Instant Pot

## Ingredients

- 1 cup (200 g) black rice
- 2 Tbsp (50 g) sweet rice
- 1 cup water
- 1/4 cup coconut milk

## Instructions

1. Wash black rice and sweet rice
2. Add rice, water, and coconut milk to Instant Pot
3. Pressure cook on High for 18 minutes
4. Allow natural release for 10 minutes
