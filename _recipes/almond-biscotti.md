## Equipment

- Hand Mixer

## Ingredients

### Dry
- 2 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 1 cup almonds, whole

### Cream Ingredients
- 3 Tbsp butter, softened
- 7/8 cup sugar
- 1 Tbsp olive oil
- 2 eggs
- 1/4 tsp vanilla extract
- 3/4 tsp almond extract

## Instructions

1. Using a hand mixer, beat sugar, softened butter, and olive oil until creamy and smooth
2. Add eggs, vanilla extract, and almond extract
3. Combine dry ingredients, add to mixture
4. Add almonds
5. Fridge for 30 minutes or until dough is workable
6. Preheat oven to 350°F
7. Divide the dough into two logs, rectangle in shape
> First Bake
8. Bake on a Silpat for 30 minutes or until the top just turns golden and a toothpick comes out clean
9. Cool 15 minutes on a wire rack
> Second Bake
10. Cut the biscotti diagonally (not too wide) using a serrated knife
11. Bake at 300°F to finish drying out the biscotti. 
12. Cool completely on a wire rack before storage
