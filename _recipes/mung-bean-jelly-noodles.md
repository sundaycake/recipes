## Ratio
Use a 1:7 ratio of mung bean starch to water

## Ingredients

- 1 cup mung bean starch + 7 cups water
- 1 tsp salt

## Instructions

1. Mix starch and small amount of cold water in a bowl
2. In a pot, add remaining water and heat until small bubbles appear
3. Add starch mixture to pot
4. Over medium low heat, stir constantly until it becomes gluestick consistency 1-2 minutes
5. It's done when it is mostly gray translucent and smooth
6. Transfer to glass Pyrex container and cool on the counter 1-2 hours
7. Place in fridge to cool
