## Chicken Leg Roasters
- Brown chicken legs about 2 minutes each side
- 22 minutes on High + 10 minutes natural release

## Eggs, hard boiled
- 10 ounces water (RO)
- 4 minutes on High + Quick release

## Pork (Chile verde, Carnitas, etc)
- 25 minutes on High + 15 minutes natural release

## Boba
- 20 minutes on Low + 20 minutes natural release

## Porridge
- 1 cup rice + 5 cups water
- 15 minutes on High/Porridge + 15 minutes natural release
