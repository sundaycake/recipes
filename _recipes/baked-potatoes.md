## Instructions

### Microwave
1. Wash potato, pierce several times with fork 
2. Microwave on plate uncovered on High
3. Total time is 4-5 minutes, add 2-3 minutes for each additional potato
4. Flip potato halfway through
5. Potato is ready  when internal temperature is 180°F to 190°F
6. Go to the next air fryer step

### Air Fryer
1. Air fry for 10-12 minutes at 400°F
2. Potato is ready to eat when  internal temperature is 205°F to 210°F
