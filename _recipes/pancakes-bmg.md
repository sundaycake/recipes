## Equipment

* Cast iron pans

## Ingredients

- 50 g oat flour
- 30 g flax seed meal 
- 170 g all-purpose flour

### Substitution
- 2 cups all-purpose flour (250 g)

## Instructions

1. Make buttermilk
2. In a large bowl, mix dry ingredients
3. In a separate bowl, mix wet ingredients
4. Gently fold wet ingredients into dry

## Substitutions

Buttermilk substitutes (makes ~1 cup):
- 1 Tbsp lemon juice or white vinegar + 1 cup (240 g) milk
- 1 Tbsp lemon juice or white vinegar + 1 cup (240 g) water + 75 g milk powder
- 1 Tbsp lemon juice or white vinegar + 1/2 cup (120 g) evaporated milk + 1/2 cup (120 g) water
