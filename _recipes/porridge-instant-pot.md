## Rice
1 "cup" of rice is 3/4 cup customary units

## Equipment

Instant Pot

## Ingredients

- 1 cup rice, rinsed
- 5 cups water
- 2 chicken drumsticks or thighs

### Aromatics
- grated ginger
- white pepper
- salt to taste
- chopped scallions

## Instructions

1. Rinse rice, add water and chicken to pot
2. Use Porridge setting in Instant Pot
3. Cook for 15 minutes + 15 minutes natural release
4. After releasing steam, set to Saute and add aromatics and shred chicken meat
