## Equipment
- Hand mixer
- Aebleskiver pan


## Ingredients

### Dry Ingredients
- 2 cups all purpose flour
- 1 tablespoon granulated sugar
- 1/2 teaspoons salt
- 2 teaspoons baking powder
- 1/2 teaspoons baking soda

### Wet Ingredients
- 2 egg whites
- 2 egg yolks
- 4 tablespoons butter, melted
- 2 cups buttermilk


## Instructions
1. Make stiff peaks with egg whites
2. Mix everything except egg whites together
3. Fold egg whites into batter
4. Cook using toothpicks in aebleskiver pan
