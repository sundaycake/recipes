## Equipment
- 9" x 5" loaf pan, greased

## Ingredients

### Dry ingredients

- 1 3/4 cup flour
- 1 cup sugar
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 1/2 tsp salt
- Optional: 1/2 cup chopped walnuts and/or 1/2 cup chocolate chips (100g)

Wet Ingredients:
1 cup mashed bananas (2-3 overripe bananas)
2 eggs, lightly beaten
1/3 cup milk
1/3 cup vegetable oil

## Instructions

1. Mix wet ingredients, set aside
2. Sift and combine dry ingredients
3. Add wet ingredients into dry, do not overmix
4. Add optional ingredients
5. Pour batter into pan
6. Bake for 1 hour and 20 minutes at 325 degrees
7. Test for doneness with a toothpick, do not overbake
