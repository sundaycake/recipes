## Ingredients

### Dry
- 850 g flour
- 400 g sugar
- 2 1/4 tsp salt 
- 3 tablespoons baking powder

### Wet
- 3/4 cups vegetable oil + 1 tsp vegetable oil
- 8 tablespoons butter (melted)
- 4 eggs
- 1.5 cups milk (room temperature) + 1 Tbsp milk
 
### Toppings
- 4 1/2 cups blueberry/cranberry or any extra fillings

### Optional Crumb Topping
- 100 g granulated sugar
- 90 g all-purpose flour
- 70 g unsalted butter

### Lemon Muffin variation changes:
- 1/4 cup vegetable oil (replaces standard recipe vegetable oil)
- 200 g lemon juice
- 8 g lemon/citrus zest
- 100mL lemon juice + 50 g powdered sugar (glaze, reduce in saucepan)
- Instructions: Bake 5 mins at 380, 10 minutes at 350, broil at 3:30 for 5 minutes or until golden brown

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 380 degrees F
2. Combine flour, sugar, salt and baking powder
3. Place vegetable oil into measuring cup; add the egg and add enough milk to reach the 4.5-cup mark
4. Mix well
5. Add to flour mixture; add filling; don't over mix
6. Fill muffin cups
7. Bake in oven, after 5 minutes reduce heat to 350 degrees F 
8. Bake for 20 to 25 minutes until done
9. Check around 15 minute mark  

## Notes

Try not to exceed 20 minutes baking time. Don't lower temperature below 350 and add heat shield on bottom if needed

## Tips

> Favorite filling:
- lemon zest and diced apricot with lemon sugar glaze (lemon juice and sugar condensed)
- blueberry
- cranberry
- chocolate chip
- crystallized ginger and lemon zest with lemon sugar glaze.
