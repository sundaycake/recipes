## Equipment



## Ingredients
- 1 to 2 large cabbages
- kosher or sea salt (do not use iodized salt)

## Instructions
1. Shared cabbage, massage salt into shredded cabbage
2. After 30-60 minutes, put shredded cabbage into clean sterilized mason jar
3. Pour excess water to top off jar, do not overfill. Reserve one large leaf for sauerkraut cap
4. Ferment at room temperature for 20 days in summer, 30 days in winter. Fridge when ready


## Notes
To calculate how much salt to use, use 25 g salt for every 1000 g cabbage
