## Equipment

Instant Pot

## Ingredients

- 1 cup mung bean, peeled and split
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 1 1/3 cups water

## Instructions

1. Rinse mung beans
2. Add mung beans, salt, and water to Instant Pot
3. Pressure cook for 2 minutes Manual on High Pressure
4. Allow 10-15 minutes for natural release
5. Mash mung bean

## Notes

- If making sweet filling, add sugar and oil
