## Equipment

1. Hand blender or stand mixer
2. See Notes for pan size

## Ingredients

### Dry
1 1/2 cups (150 g) almond flour
3/4 cup (64 g) cocoa powder
1 teaspoon baking powder
1/2 teaspoon salt

### Wet
5 tablespoons (70 g) butter softened
3 large eggs
1 teaspoon vanilla extract
Scant 1 1/2 cups (280 g) granulated or brown sugar (see Tips)

Optional: 1/3 cup (115 g) chocolate chips, optional

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 350F. Grease your baking pan with butter/flour, or use parchment paper and set aside.
2. Mix dry ingredients, set aside
3. In a separate bowl:
- Whisk eggs and sugar for 1-2 minutes until sugar is mostly dissolved
- Add melted butter, gradually whisk into egg-sugar mixture until fully emulsified
- Add vanilla to the wet mixture
4. Fold in homogenized dry ingredients just until combined, preserving the aerated egg-sugar structure
5. Fold in chocolate chips last, minimally
6. Transfer brownie batter into pan. Bake for 35 minutes, or until a skewer comes out clean.
7. Remove from the oven and allow to cool. Loosen the edges but do not cut brownie yet
8. Place brownie pan in fridge and wait until completely cold before dividing into squares

## Notes

Recipe scaling:
1. 1x use 8x8 pan
2. 1.5x use 9x13 pan

## Tips

* Brown sugar makes brownie more chewy. Granulated sugar is slightly better for whisking and shiny top
* Shiny brownie top science: The glossy layer forms from sugar dissolving into the egg's liquid/protein phase and rising to the surface

## Substitutions

## Adaptation

Reduce sugar, reduce chocolate chips, add optional brown sugar, change directions for shiny brownie top
