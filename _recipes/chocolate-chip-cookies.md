## Ingredients

- 2½ cups all-purpose flour (300 grams)
- 1 teaspoon baking soda (5 grams)
- 1 cup unsalted butter (8 ounces / 227 grams)
- ¾ cup granulated sugar (150 grams)
- ¾ cup brown sugar (165 grams)
- 2 large eggs
- 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
- 2 cups chocolate chips (12 ounces / 340 grams)

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C).
2. Combine the flour and baking soda.
3. Beat the butter and sugars until light and fluffy.
4. Add the eggs and vanilla.
5. Gradually mix in the dry ingredients.
6. Fold in the chocolate chips.
7. Bake for 10–12 minutes, until the edges are golden brown.

## Notes

Allow the cookies to cool on the baking sheet for 5 minutes before transferring them to a rack.
