## Tapioca mini pearls
- 1:3 or 1:4 ratio of pearls to water (1/2 cup pearls to 2 cups water)
- Boil uncovered on low heat for 10 minutes
- Turn off heat, cover with lid, let stand for 10 minutes
- Rinse with cold water

## Chickpea (dried)
- 1:4 ratio of chickpeas to water (1 cup chickpeas to 4 cups water)
- Instant Pot: 5 minutes high + 25 minutes natural release
- Stove top: 1 hour

## Beans, pinto (dried)
- Instant Pot:
- Stove top: 2 hours

## Barley
- 1:2 ratio of barley to water (1 cup barley to 2 cups water)

## Oatmeal (Coach's)
- Instant Pot: 1:4 ratio of oatmeal to water (1 cup oatmeal to 4 cups water)
- Instant Pot: Porridge setting 6 minutes high, 4 minutes natural release
- Stove top: 1:2 ratio of oatmeal to water (1 cup oatmeal to 2 cups water)

## Quinoa
- 1:1.75 ratio of quinoa to water (1 cup quinoa to 1 3/4 cups water)
