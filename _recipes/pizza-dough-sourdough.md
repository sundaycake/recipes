## Equipment
Bread Machine


## Ingredients
- 290 g poolish
- 150 g water
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 2 cups bread flour
- 2 teaspoons salt
- 3 g sugar

## Instructions
1. Combine everything except poolish and autolyse for 30 minutes
2. Then add levain to dough and add to Panasonic bread machine and select the Pizza cycle (45 minutes)
3. When done, remove dough and divide into two equal balls
4. Place dough into oiled bowl, cover and let rise for 3-5 hours or into fridge for cold ferment. If using from cold ferment, allow to proof on counter covered for 2-3 hours before shaping


## Notes
Gluten strands broke down a lot, consider giving the starter a feeding before making the poolish
