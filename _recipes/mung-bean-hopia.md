## Equipment



## Ingredients

### Filling
- 1 cup mung beans or red beans
- 3/4 cup light brown sugar
- 1/4 cup sweetened condensed milk
- 1/4 cup all purpose flour

### Water Dough
- 1 cup all purpose flour
- 1 tablespoon granulated sugar
- 1/4 cup water
- 1/4 cup unsalted butter OR shortening
- 1/8 teaspoon kosher salt

### Oil Dough
- 1/2 cup all purpose flour
- 1/4 cup unsalted butter OR shortening

### Egg wash
- 1 medium egg
- 1/4 cup water

## Instructions

1. In a medium bowl, combine the water dough ingredients 
2. In a different bowl, combine the oil dough ingredients
3. Flatten the water dough with a rolling pin and form a rectangle 10 x 12 inches wide and set aside
4. Roll out and flatten the oil dough to measure 1/3 of the water dough. Place in the center of the water dough and letter fold (right third over middle, left third over middle)
5. Repeat these folds for a total of three folds, then rest for 5 minutes.
6. Roll dough into rectangle and divide in two. Roll each dough into a cylinder and cut into two rounds.
7. Preheat oven to 350 degrees.
8. Roll the cylinder into a round, and place the filling in the middle. Pinch the dough together to seal the filling and place seam side down
9. Bake seam side up for 15-20 minutes, then flip, brush with egg wash, and bake seam side down for 5-10 minutes.
