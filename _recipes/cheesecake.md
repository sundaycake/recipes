## Ingredients

### Graham cracker crust
- 3/4 cups graham crackers
- 3 Tbsp butter, melted

### Biscoff crust
- 125 g package of biscoff
- 2 Tbsp butter, melted

### Filling
- 2 packages cream cheese, room temp (Defrost Fish cycle: 0.3 pounds)
- 1/2 cup sugar
- 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
- 2 eggs
- 1 Tbsp lemon juice
- 1/4 tsp citric acid

## Instructions

### Bake crust
1. Make crust by combining graham crackers and melted butter
2. Press into greased pan
3. Bake at 325°F for 10 minutes

### Make Filling
1. Use electric mixer and beat sugar, cream cheese, vanilla extract, lemon juice, and citric acid
2. Hand mix eggs in one at a time
3. Pour mixture into crust
4. (Optional) Add marbling
5. Bake 30-35 minutes at 325°F or 60 minutes at 250°F or until center reaches 145°F
6. (Optional) Add strawberries and/or mirror glaze/Raspberry Glaze
7. Refrigerate overnight

## Notes

- Remember to alternate chocolate and plain when pouring it into the pan, and mix the chocolate into 3/4 cup (per batch) of batter. 
- Used 8g of finely ground coffee. 

## Tips

> Bake 35-40 minutes with water bath, turn off oven and let residual heat finish cooking
