## Equipment

- Instant Pot

## Ingredients

- 1 cup black eyed peas
- 1 cup sticky rice
- 6 cups water
- 1/2 cup sugar
- A dash of vanilla extract

### Coconut topping
- Coconut milk
- Cornstarch
- Water

## Instructions

1. Combine all ingredients except coconut toppings into instant pot
2. Cook 18 minutes on high, 5 minutes rest then natural release
3. For softer peas: 18 minutes on high, 10 minutes rest then natural release
