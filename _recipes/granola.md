# Granola

## summary 
* Active time: 30 minutes, Inactive time: ~3 hours  
* 12 servings

> Bake at 300°F (150°C) 100 minutes  

## ingredients
> Single Batch
> One 13x18 pan

* 3 1/2 cups (350 g) rolled oats  
* 3 tsp (12 g) chia seeds  
* 1 cup (240 g) buttermilk (1 Tbsp vinegar and 1 cup milk, see Notes for substitutes)  
* 8 Tbsp (115 g) melted butter  
* 3/4 cup (150 g) sugar  
* 1/8 tsp sea salt  
* 1/2 cup almonds, chopped  
* 1/2 cup dried cranberries  
* 1/2 cup dark raisins  

> Double Batch
> Two 13x18 pans

7 cups (700 g) rolled oats  
6 tsp (24 g) chia seeds  
2 cups (480 g) buttermilk (2 Tbsp vinegar and 2 cups milk, see Notes for substitutes)  
16 Tbsp (230 g) melted butter  
1 1/2 cups (300 g) sugar  
1/4 tsp sea salt  
1 cup almonds, chopped  
1 cup dried cranberries  
1 cup dark raisins  

## instructions 

1. Make buttermilk (see Notes below), set aside for 20 minutes while assembling ingredients
2. Combine buttermilk with oats, chia, and melted butter
2. Let mixture rest for 20 minutes or until oats are stiff
3. Add salt and sugar, rest for 30 minutes up to a few hours
4. Preheat oven to 300°F (150°C), middle rack
5. Spread granola mix on silicone mat or parchment paper on 13x18 pan
6. Stir granola mix every 20 minutes, total baking time roughly 100 minutes
7. 15 minutes before granola is done, add nuts and dried fruit
8. (Optional) Turn off oven, allow granola to rest for 1 hour for maximum crunchiness

Store in airtight jar for up to 6 weeks at room temperature

## notes

Buttermilk substitutes (makes ~1 cup):
- 1 Tbsp lemon juice or white vinegar + 1 cup (240 g) milk
- 1 Tbsp lemon juice or white vinegar + 1 cup (240 g) water + 75 g milk powder
- 1 Tbsp lemon juice or white vinegar + 1/2 cup (120 g) evaporated milk + 1/2 cup (120 g) water
