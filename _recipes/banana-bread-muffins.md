## Equipment
- 12 count muffin tin

## Ingredients

### Dry ingredients

- 260 grams flour
- 150 grams sugar
- 1 1/2 tablespoons baking powder
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- Optional: 1/2 cup chopped walnuts and/or 1/2 cup chocolate chips

### Wet Ingredients:
- 1 cup mashed bananas (2-3 overripe bananas)
- 2 eggs, lightly beaten
- 1/3 cup milk
- 1/3 cup vegetable oil
- 2 Tablespoons melted butter

## Instructions

1. Mix wet ingredients, set aside
2. Sift and combine dry ingredients
3. Add wet ingredients into dry, do not overmix
4. Add optional ingredients
5. Pour batter into muffin tray
6. Bake for 22-25 minutes at 375 degrees
